# Product brief

## What this is

**THE UNORTHODOX PATH** — a single-page personal portfolio for an AI/ML engineer and
software developer.

## Who it is for

| Audience | What they need within a minute |
| --- | --- |
| Technical recruiters, hiring managers | Role, evidence of real projects, a way to make contact |
| Engineering teams | Technical depth and judgement shown in the work and the build itself |
| Freelance clients | Confidence in competence and design quality |

## What it must communicate

- Technical depth without unnecessary complexity.
- Individuality without losing professionalism.
- Quiet confidence, discipline, progression, craft.

## Identity

Murim and manhwa inform the art direction — ink and paper, negative space, a deliberate
path — but are never the literal subject. The site is not a game, a cultivation dashboard,
or a HUD.

## Scope of mission 001 (delivered)

- Global shell: sticky header, mobile menu, footer, skip link.
- Hero with the identity, role statement, and a route into the work.
- Selected work: three project entries built from a typed content model.
- "Further chapters": honest placeholders that own the anchors for the remaining sections.
- Design tokens, typography, motion foundation.

## Section architecture

| # | Section | Anchor | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Hero / introduction | `#top` | Live |
| 02 | Selected projects | `#work` | Live, facts thin |
| 03 | About & engineering approach | `#approach` | Planned |
| 04 | Technical capabilities | `#capabilities` | Planned |
| 05 | Résumé & professional links | `#resume` | Planned |
| 06 | Contact | `#contact` | Planned |

Work is placed before About so evidence comes first. Revisit once the About copy exists.

## Open decisions for the owner

- Real name, and whether the brand or the name leads the hero.
- Contact destination (email and/or form) and professional links.
- Whether case studies become separate routes (`/work/[slug]`) or stay on the page.
- Hosting and domain. Nothing is deployed.
